# Your library in the sidebar

The Earworm icon in the activity bar opens your queue, recently played songs, liked songs, playlists and saved albums. Click a song to play it, or **+** to add it to the queue.

The buttons at the top of the Library:

- **Search Spotify**: songs, albums and playlists as you type
- **Choose Spotify Device**: move playback to your phone, a speaker or another computer
- **Refresh Library**

Click the heart in the status bar to like or unlike the song that's playing.

Spotify shows the songs only of playlists you own or collaborate on. The others still play when you click them.
