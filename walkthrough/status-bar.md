# Make the status bar yours

Tick only the items you want: previous, play/pause, next, the song name, the heart and the progress bar.

- The progress bar hides while paused. Tick **Progress bar while paused** to keep it.
- Earworm can sit on the left or the right side of the status bar.

The same choices are in the **Status Bar** section of the Earworm sidebar, and in the `earworm.statusBar.items` and `earworm.statusBar.alignment` settings.
